NOVA TECH SOLUTIONS
Refund and Cancellation Policy

General Policy

NovaTech Solutions aims to provide services according to the agreed project scope and requirements.

Refund eligibility depends on the stage of the project and the work that has already been completed.

Before Development Begins

If a client cancels a project before development has started, the company may provide a refund of the amount paid after deducting any non-refundable third-party costs.

After Development Begins

Once development has started, payments may not be fully refundable because resources and development time have already been allocated to the project.

If a project is cancelled after development begins, NovaTech Solutions will evaluate the completed work and costs before determining any applicable refund.

Completed Projects

Payments for completed and approved projects are generally non-refundable.

Third-Party Costs

Payments made to third-party services, software providers, hosting providers, domain registrars, or other external providers may not be refundable.

Scope Changes

Requests that substantially change the original project scope may require additional payment and may affect the project timeline.

Disputes

Clients should first contact NovaTech Solutions to discuss any billing or project concerns. The company will review the issue and attempt to reach a reasonable resolution.
