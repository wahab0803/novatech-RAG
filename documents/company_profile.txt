NOVA TECH SOLUTIONS
Company Profile

NovaTech Solutions is a software and technology company that helps small and medium-sized businesses improve their operations through software, websites, AI solutions, and business automation.

The company was founded in 2022 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas, USA.

NovaTech Solutions primarily works with small businesses, startups, professional service companies, and e-commerce businesses.

Our main goal is to reduce repetitive manual work and help businesses use technology to improve their daily operations.

Company Services

NovaTech Solutions provides the following services:

1. Website Development
We design and develop responsive business websites, landing pages, portfolio websites, and e-commerce websites.

2. AI Chatbots
We develop AI-powered chatbots that can answer customer questions using a company's own information and knowledge base.

3. Business Automation
We automate repetitive business processes such as lead collection, customer notifications, appointment reminders, data entry, and internal workflows.

4. Custom Software Development
We develop custom software applications based on a company's specific requirements.

5. AI Integration
We integrate AI models and APIs into existing business applications and workflows.

6. SaaS Development
We design and develop software-as-a-service applications for startups and businesses.

NovaTech Solutions does not currently provide dedicated SEO services, paid advertising management, social media management, or graphic design as standalone services.
